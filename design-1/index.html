What we do

A broad technical scope, clearly organised.

The Icon Alliance company profile covers engineering consultancy, installation and commissioning, fire and electrical systems, power infrastructure, solar, security, maintenance and material supply.

Fire & life safety

Fire protection & detection

Fire safety planning, detection, alarm, sprinkler, hydrant and suppression systems, together with safety equipment and licensing support.

  • Fire safety plans, fire-main layouts, emergency exit maps and hydrant routes; BFSCD approval and licensing support
  • Addressable alarm panels, smoke and heat detectors, manual call points, sounders and beacons
  • Sprinkler, fire hydrant, fire door and inert-gas / clean-agent suppression systems
  • CO₂, dry-chemical and foam extinguishers, hoses, fire blankets and safety equipment

Electrical engineering

Electrical design & installation

Electrical and fire-related design, drawings, consultancy and audit, followed by installation, testing and commissioning.

  • Residential and industrial wiring
  • Electrical and fire design, drawings and technical consultancy
  • Installation, testing and commissioning
  • Lightning Protection System (LPS)

Power & distribution

Substations, switchgear & generators

Power infrastructure, transformers, generator systems, distribution boards and industrial control panels.

  • Substations, oil / dry-type transformers and diesel generators
  • HT / LT switchgear, MDB / SDB, ATS, PFI and motor-control panels
  • AVR, VFD, inverters, motors, sensors and relays
  • Circuit breakers, magnetic contactors, DOL starters, control equipment and generator spare parts

Renewable energy

Solar energy systems

Solar solutions for different site and power requirements, with on-grid, off-grid and hybrid configurations.

  • On-grid, off-grid and hybrid systems
  • Solar PV modules, commercial inverters and mounting systems
  • Optional energy storage and backup power
  • System monitoring through app / web

Security & communication

Security, access & PA systems

CCTV, access control and public-address solutions for communication, announcements and voice evacuation.

  • CCTV / camera systems, access control and two-way radio communication
  • PA and sound system design, installation, testing and commissioning
  • Voice Evacuation (VA), inspection and preventive maintenance
  • Troubleshooting, repair, system upgrades and 24/7 PA technical support

Maintenance & supply

Maintenance, signage & spare parts

Planned support for installed systems, alongside spare parts and fire- and engineering-related material supply.

  • Monthly Maintenance Contracts (MMC) and Yearly Maintenance Contracts (YMC)
  • Preventative operations support, inspection, troubleshooting and repair
  • System upgrades, expansion and after-sales service
  • Generator spare parts, signage / digital signage and engineering material supply

Services and equipment are provided according to the agreed project scope and site requirements.
